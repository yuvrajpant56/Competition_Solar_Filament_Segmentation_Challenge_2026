
The Winning Recipe for Small Filaments

To win this competition, your pipeline should focus on feature preservation and structural continuity rather than just a massive backbone.

1. Core Architecture: UNet++ with a Specialized Encoder

• Why: The nested, dense skip pathways in UNet++ bridge the semantic gap between encoder and decoder feature maps, preventing the fine details of small filaments from getting lost during downsampling.
• The Fix: Use a Timm-EfficientNet-B5 or ResNeXt-50 backbone pre-trained on high-resolution structures. Do not use an overly deep backbone (like ResNet152) or fine features will blur out in deep layers.

2. Advanced Augmentation Stack (Crucial for Sparsity)

Because the background dominates the images, heavy, structure-preserving augmentations are mandatory:
• Elastic Transformations & Grid Distortion: Mimics physical solar atmospheric shearing without breaking structural continuity.
• Random Crop & Scale (Patch-Based Training): Train on smaller, high-resolution patches (e.g., 256 × 256 or 512 × 512) instead of downsampling the full solar disk. This forces the network to focus on filament micro-textures.
• Mixup / CutMix: Helps regularize the network against predicting empty backgrounds.

3. Loss Functions: Tackling the Class Imbalance

Standard Cross-Entropy will fail because the model will optimize by predicting 100% background. You must use a composite loss:
\(\text{Total\ Loss}=\alpha \cdot \text{Focal\ Loss}+\beta \cdot \text{Dice\ Loss}+\gamma \cdot \text{Topological/Lovász-Hinge Loss}\)
• Focal Loss: Down-weights easy-to-classify background pixels and forces focus on hard foreground filament pixels.
• Lovász-Hinge Loss: Directly optimizes the Intersection over Union (IoU) proxy, which aligns closely with the competition’s Panoptic Quality (PQ) evaluation metric.

4. Post-Processing & Ensemble Strategy

• Test-Time Augmentation (TTA): Average the predictions across horizontal/vertical flips and 90-degree rotations.
• Morphological Closing & Connected Components: Use dilation followed by erosion to stitch together fragmented filament predictions before calculating your final submission mask.
• Multi-Model Fusion: Ensemble a UNet++ (EfficientNet encoder) with a SegFormer (Transformer-based) model to combine strong local edge features with global spatial context.
